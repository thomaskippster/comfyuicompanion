* **Kanal:** [SECourses on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@SECourses)

* **Fokus:** Extrem detaillierte, technische ComfyUI-Guides und System-Optimierungen.

**Vorlage:**
```text
Subject: Open-Source tool for ComfyUI: Cold Storage Archiving & Missing Model Auto-Downloader

Dear Dr. Furkan,

Thank you for your outstanding, high-quality ComfyUI and Stable Diffusion tutorials. I have learned a lot from your channel!

I wanted to introduce a free, open-source tool I developed that might be very interesting to your community: **ComfyUI Companion**.

With large modern models like Flux.1 and SDXL taking up 20GB+ each, NVMe storage runs out quickly, and hunting missing models when loading complex community workflows is a frequent pain point.

Key features:
1. 1-Click Canvas Bridge: A native rocket button right inside the ComfyUI canvas syncs the active workflow and checks for missing/archived models.
2. Cold Storage Archive: Move heavy 10-30GB checkpoints to a slower HDD and restore/symlink them in 1-click when an imported workflow requires them.
3. 100% Local AI (Gemma 2B via CPU): Reconstructs missing model names and queries Civitai/HuggingFace automatically without any cloud API keys.
4. AutoV1 Fast Hashing: 100MB chunk hashing to find duplicates in seconds.
5. Easy deployment: Available via 1-click Pinokio or pre-compiled JAR.

- Repository: https://github.com/thomaskippster/comfyuicompanion
- Demo video: https://github.com/thomaskippster/comfyuicompanion/raw/main/assets/comfyuicompanion.mp4

If you find it useful, feel free to showcase it or mention it to your viewers. I would be thrilled to hear your thoughts!

Best regards,
Thomas
```
